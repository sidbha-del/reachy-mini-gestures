Reachy Mini Gestures

Point, wave or give a thumbs up. Reachy Mini answers with its head, body and antennas.

Gestures

  • ☝️PointHead and body follow your finger
  • ☝️☝️Two pointersAntennas copy your finger angles
  • 👋HelloWaves back

  • 👎Thumbs downSad head shake

  • 👉Bang!Falls down dead, then comes back

  • ✊✊Ta-daTwo fists, then open hands
  • 🙏NamasteBows with antennas together

Get started

  1. Turn on Reachy Mini and start its daemon (the Reachy Mini dashboard does this for you).
  2. In the dashboard's app list, find reachy_mini_gestures, install it and press start. Or install from source: git clone https://github.com/sidbha-del/reachy-mini-gestures, then pip install -e . and run reachy-mini-gestures.
  3. Open https://localhost:8765 and accept the browser's certificate warning once (the app makes its own local certificate).
  4. Choose a camera: the robot's, your laptop's, or your phone standing in front of Reachy.

This app controls a real robot, so it runs on the computer connected to Reachy Mini, not on Hugging Face. Full setup, phone camera and troubleshooting help is in the README.
